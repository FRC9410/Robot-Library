# Timeline Notes and Bookmarks

**Status:** Proposed, 2026-10-30.

## Idea
Add notes at points on the timeline ("intake jammed here"). They're saved next to the log file,
so they show up whenever the log is opened again.

## Why
Keeps what the drive team saw attached to the data.
