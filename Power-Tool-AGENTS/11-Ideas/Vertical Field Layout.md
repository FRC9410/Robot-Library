# Vertical Field Layout

**Status:** Proposed, 2026-11-02, by the team's mentor. Partly tried the same day: the vertical field (driver side at the bottom) is built and used in the Logs tab's **3 columns** layout (Power card, field, graphs), which the team chose as the only layout on 2026-11-02. The 2-column version with the field on the right and fully expanded rows isn't built.

The mentor also suggested a switch between the power graph and the table ("bonus points"); not built.

## Idea
A different layout for the Logs tab:
- The field turned vertical on the right side of the screen, with the driver station side at the bottom.
- On the left, the power draw graph and the subsystem table together in one card.
- No expanders in the table: every subsystem's rows are always fully expanded.

## Why
The field uses a lot of width when it's horizontal. Standing it up on the right gives the left side
more room for the power graph and table, and puts the field the way the driver sees it.
