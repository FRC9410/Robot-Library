# Match Summary

**Status:** Proposed, 2026-10-30.

## Idea
An auto-generated card for each match showing battery low point, peak currents, cycle count, auto
result and any [Problem Markers](Problem%20Markers.md). The cards can be compared across a whole event.

## Why
Spots trends, like a battery or a mechanism getting worse over a day.

## Open questions
- "Cycle count" depends on the game. Could it come from a state change, e.g. counts of `ActualState` entering a scoring state?
